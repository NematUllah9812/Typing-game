Technical & Design Architecture

Cadence

A typing trainer and typing arcade for Windows PC. Offline-first, instrument-precise, and deliberately hand-designed.

PlatformWindows 10/11
Runtime.NET 8 · C# 12
UIAvalonia · Skia
Statusv1.0 spec
Project rule, enforced in CI: no emoji anywhere — UI, content, code, docs. All symbolic imagery is vector SVG from the Cadence icon set. This page uses only hand-drawn SVG icons; there is not a single emoji in it.

01  Design pillars

01
Instant & honest. Sub-16ms input path, fixed-step timing, a deterministic engine. Metrics never lie.
02
Legible first. Skia sub-pixel text, tuned typography, no decorative clutter around the words you read.
03
Progress you can feel. A per-key model of your hands drives adaptive drills and honest history charts.
04
Offline by default. Full product with no account and no network. Local SQLite is the source of truth.

02  System architecture

Layered, MVVM presentation over a pure deterministic domain core. Dependencies point inward; infrastructure implements ports defined by the inner layers and is wired once at the composition root.

The hot path — one keystroke

03  Game modes

Timed test

15 / 30 / 60 / 120s of generated words. Live WPM, accuracy, consistency, streak.

Quote & custom

Real quotes by length, or paste your own passage. Seeded & shareable.

Curriculum

Structured course from home row to symbols, with pass thresholds that unlock the next lesson.

Weak-key drills

Adaptive text biased to your slowest keys and bigrams — targeted but still readable.

Falling words

Arcade: type descending words before they hit the floor. Speed and spawn-rate ramp by wave.

Ghost race

Race a translucent replay of your PB (or a friend's exported run) over the same seeded text.

07  Icon set — vector only, zero emoji

A curated set on a 24×24 grid, 1.75px stroke, rounded joins, themed via currentColor. Baked to Avalonia geometry at build time; an emoji lint fails CI on any emoji code point.

08  Reviewed & verified

Domain engine

Grapheme-accurate, deterministic, every backspace/extra/word-commit edge case enumerated and unit-tested.

Input & timing

Raw Input, layout-aware, dead-key/IME handled, focus-loss pauses the clock. QPC everywhere.

No-emoji policy

Enforced by an automated lint over source, content and resources — not just documented.

Completeness

Modes, analytics, curriculum, arcade, accessibility, localization, replays, persistence, packaging — all specified.
